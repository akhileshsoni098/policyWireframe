1 Policy Kya Hai?

Policy = Insurance ka Contract

Policy ek legal agreement hai jo insurer (insurance company) aur insured (customer) ke beech mein hota hai. Ismein likha hota hai ki kaun se risk cover hain, kitni limit hai, kitna deductible hai, aur kitna premium dena hai.

Example: POL-2026-001 = ABC Construction Inc. ki General Liability policy. Ismein $2M limit hai, $10K deductible hai, aur $125,000 annual premium hai. 1 year ka term hai — 1 Jan 2026 se 31 Dec 2026 tak.

Policy ke 5 important parts:

  • Policy Number — Unique identifier, jaise POL-2026-001
  • Status — Active / Pending / Cancelled / Expired / Renewed
  • Term — Kitni der coverage hai (6 ya 12 months)
  • Premium — Kitna paisa dena hai
  • Insured — Policy ka malik (person ya company)
Flow: Submission → Quote → Bind → Issue → Active Policy → Endorsement/Renewal/Cancellation

2 Policy Term aur Version

Term = Policy ka Time Period

Har policy ka ek Term hota hai — effective date se expiration date tak. Jaise POL-2026-001 ka Term 1 hai: 2026-01-01 se 2026-12-31. Jab policy renew hoti hai toh naya term banta hai — Term 2, Term 3, etc.

Version har change par create hota hai. Naya business pe Version 1 banta hai. Endorsement (mid-term change) pe naya version create hota hai. Isse pata chalta hai ki policy kab aur kaise change hui — poori history track hoti hai.

Example — Versions:
POL-2026-004 (Coastal Properties)
Ver 1 — New Business (01-Jan-2026)
Ver 2 — Endorsement: Limit Increase (15-Mar-2026)
Ver 3 — Endorsement: AI Add (10-Jun-2026)
Example — Terms:
POL-2026-001 (ABC Construction)
Term 1: 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31
Term 2: 2027-01-01 to 2027-12-31 (Renewed)
Term 3: 2028-01-01 to 2028-12-31 (Renewed)

3 Complete PAS Journey — 9 Steps

1. Submission (Application Dalna)

Broker ya agent insured ki taraf se ek submission create karta hai. Ismein insured ka naam, business type, revenue, loss history, aur required coverage details hoti hain. System isse New Submission status mein daal deta hai aur UW Queue mein bhej deta hai.

New Submission UW Queue Quote Bind
Ramesh Bhai ka Example: Marsh Inc. ne ABC Construction ki submission create ki — $5M revenue, 3 employees, General Liability + Auto coverage, $2M limit. Status: New Submission

2. Quote Generation (Rate Banana)

Submission ke basis par system ek quote generate karta hai. Quote mein base premium, experience mod, schedule credit, taxes, fees sab calculate hote hain. 5-step wizard mein insured info, coverage selection, details, premium breakdown aur review hota hai. Quote approve hone ke baad hi UW queue mein aage process hota hai.

Example — Blue Ridge Manufacturing: Base $72,500 × Mod 0.92 × Credit 95% + Taxes $3,550 + Fees $1,200 = Total $87,502

3. Underwriting Review (Risk Evaluation)

UW Queue mein submission aata hai. Yahan Underwriter risk ko evaluate karta hai — loss history, financials, score (1-10 scale). Teen decisions hote hain:

  • Approve — Risk acceptable hai, policy bind kar sakte hain
  • Refer — Senior UW ke paas bhejna hai (score < 5)
  • Decline — Risk unacceptable, reject with reason
Business Rule: Score 5 se kam hai → Senior UW referral mandatory. Score 7+ hai → auto-approve upto $500K premium. Score 3 se kam → auto-decline.
New Submission UW Queue UW Approve Bind

4. Bind & Issue (Policy Active Banana)

UW approval ke baad policy bind hoti hai. Bind ka matlab — temporary coverage start ho gayi. Phir Issue hota hai — policy number generate hota hai (POL-2026-001), official documents PDF mein bante hain, billing schedule banta hai, aur status Active ho jata hai.

UW Approved Bind Issue Active
Ramesh Bhai ka Example: POL-2026-001 issue hui. Policy number mil gaya. Premium $125,000. 4 quarterly installments of $31,250. Status: Active

5. Active Policy (Policy Chal Rahi Hai)

Policy issue hone ke baad Active status mein aa jati hai. Ab insured ko coverage milti hai, billing schedule ke hisaab se payments hote hain, aur UW monitoring karta hai. Is stage par 3 major actions possible hain:

  • Endorsement — coverage mein mid-term change
  • Renewal — naye term ke liye extend karna
  • Cancellation — policy band karna

6. Endorsement (Mid-term Change)

Kabhi kabhi policy ke beech mein changes ki zaroorat hoti hai — jaise Additional Insured add karna, limit badhana, deductible change karna, ya location add karna. Iske liye Endorsement create karte hain. Endorsement se:

  • Premium change hota hai (additional ya return premium)
  • Billing schedule adjust hoti hai
  • Policy ka naya version banta hai (Version 2)
  • New policy documents issue hote hain
Ramesh Bhai ka Example: ABC Construction ne ABC Properties LLC ko additional insured add kiya. Endorsement create hua. Premium +$2,500 barha. Nayi annual premium $127,500. Nayi billing: 4 installments of $31,875. Version 2 bana.
Policy Active Endorsement Premium Change Billing Adjust

7. Renewal (Naya Term)

Policy ke expiration date se pehle Renewal process shuru hota hai. UW current term ke experience ko review karta hai, naya premium calculate karta hai (loss trends, inflation, experience mod change), aur naya term offer karta hai. Renewal se naya policy term aur naya version create hota hai.

Ramesh Bhai ka Renewal Example:

8. Cancellation (Policy Band Karna)

Kisi wajah se policy beech mein band karni hoti hai. Cancellation ke 3 types hote hain:

  • Flat Cancellation — Effective date se pehle cancel. No premium owed. Koi coverage nahi mila, koi paisa nahi.
  • Pro-rata Cancellation — UW-initiated (non-payment, risk change). Jitna time coverage use kiya utna premium (earned), baaki premium wapas (unearned).
  • Short Rate Cancellation — Insured ne khud cancel karaya. 10% penalty on unearned premium. Insured ko thoda kam premium wapas milta hai.
Example — POL-2026-005 (Pioneer Energy): Non-payment ke karan UW ne cancel kiya. 7 months coverage use hui. Earned premium $72,917. Unearned $52,083 wapas. Status: Cancelled
Policy Details Cancel Request Premium Calc Cancelled

9. Reinstatement (Wapas Active Karna)

Agar policy cancellation ke baad 30 din ke andar insured saari dues clear kar de (including late fees), toh policy ko Reinstate kiya ja sakta hai — matlab dubara Active karna. Ismein ek reinstatement fee ($250) lagti hai. Policy wapas original terms par active ho jati hai — jaise cancel hui hi nahi. Naya version banta hai indicating reinstatement.

Business Rule: Reinstatement window sirf 30 days hai. Iske baad naya submission + UW approval chahiye. Reinstatement fee $250 non-negotiable hai.
Cancelled Policy Payment Cleared Reinstate Active

4 Premium Calculation — Paise Ka Hisaab

Premium Kaise Banta Hai?

Premium simple formula se calculate hota hai:

Base Premium × Experience Mod × Schedule Credit + Taxes + Fees = Total Premium

5 Billing — Payment Ka System

Billing Plan aur Installments

Insured apna billing plan choose kar sakta hai:

  • Annual — Saara premium ek sath
  • Semi-Annual — 2 installments
  • Quarterly — 4 installments (sabse common)
  • Monthly — 12 installments (with interest)

Payment Methods: ACH (most common), Wire, Check, Credit Card (2.5% fee)

Key Rules:

  • Grace Period: Due date ke baad 10 days tak late fee nahi lagta
  • Late Fee: 10 days ke baad 1.5% monthly (18% APR)
  • Cancellation Notice: 10 days notice ke baad bhi payment nahi aayi toh cancellation start
Flow: Policy Issue → Billing Schedule Create → Installments Generate → Payment Receive → Receipt Generate → Audit Log Update

9 Har Element Par Info Button — Kyon?

Info Button (i) Ka Matlab

Screen par har field, label, table header, card, status chip ke saath ek i button hai. Yeh button teen cheezein batata hai:

  • What: Yeh element kya hai? — Definition
  • Why: Yeh kyon exist karta hai? — Business logic
  • Flow: Yeh PAS flow mein kaise connect hota hai? — Cross-module impact
Example — Policy Number (i):
What: Unique alphanumeric identifier assigned to every insurance policy. Format: POL-YYYY-NNN.
Why: Used to track, reference, and retrieve a policy across all modules — billing, claims, endorsements, renewals, and compliance reporting.
Flow: Auto-generated when a policy is bound and issued. Referenced on every transaction, document, and communication throughout the policy lifecycle.
Example — Status Badge (i):
What: Visual indicator of a policy's current state in its lifecycle. Values: Active, Pending, Cancelled, Expired, Renewed, Draft.
Why: Instantly shows where the policy stands without needing to open it. Used for filtering, reporting, and workflow decisions.
Flow: Changes automatically based on actions (Bind → Active, Cancel → Cancelled, Expire → Expired). Drives what actions are available on the policy.

10 MGA Underwriting — Delegated Authority

MGA Kya Hai?

MGA = Managing General Agent. Yeh ek specialized agent/broker hota hai jise insurance company (Carrier) ne apni taraf se underwriting karne ka delegated authority diya hota hai. Simple words mein — Carrier ne MGA ko trust kiya hai ki woh policies issue kar sakta hai, rates set kar sakta hai (limits ke andar), aur claims bhi handle kar sakta hai.

Real World Analogy: Jaise Zomato (Carrier) ne kisi local restaurant ko (MGA) authority di ho ki woh apne area mein Zomato ke brand se orders accept kar sakta hai, delivery kar sakta hai, aur issues bhi resolve kar sakta hai — bina har baar Zomato head office se permission liye.

MGA vs Traditional Broker

FeatureBrokerMGA
UW AuthorityNoneFull (within limits)
Bind CoverageCarrier se puchhna padegaKhud bind kar sakta hai
Issue PolicyCarrier karegaKhud issue kar sakta hai
Set RatesCarrier ke ratesGuidelines mein apne rates
Claims HandlingSirf report karegaUpto $50K handle kar sakta hai
Commission10-15%20-30% (profit share bhi)

MGA Ke 5 Key Roles

  • Marketing & Distribution — Carrier ke products ko market mein bechna
  • Underwriting — Risk evaluate karna aur accept/reject karna
  • Policy Administration — Policies issue karna, endorse karna, renew karna
  • Claims Management — Chhoti claims handle karna (within authority)
  • Compliance — State regulations ke hisaab se filing aur reporting

MGA Authority Levels — Kitna Power Hai?

Har MGA ko alag-alag authority milti hai. Yeh authority Binding Authority Agreement (BAA) mein likhi hoti hai. Teen levels hote hain:

LevelAuthorityBinding LimitCarrier Approval
Tier 1 — Full Bind, Issue, Endorse, Renew, Claims upto $50K Upto $1M limit / $500K premium Annual audit only
Tier 2 — Limited Bind & Issue only. Endorsements need approval Upto $500K limit / $250K premium Quarterly bordereau review
Tier 3 — Non-Binding Quote only. Bind nahi kar sakta N/A — Carrier binds directly Every submission needs UW approval
Business Rule: MGA ka authority kabhi bhi Carrier se zyada nahi ho sakta. MGA sirf utna kar sakta hai jitna Binding Authority Agreement (BAA) mein likha hai. Agar koi submission BAA se bahar hai → Refer to Carrier.

Ramesh Bhai ka Example — MGA Scenario

Maan lijiye Ramesh Bhai ki ABC Construction ko construction insurance chahiye. Lekin Southlake Insurance directly construction business nahi leti. Woh BuildSure MGA ko authority deti hai jo construction business mein expert hai.

ABC Construction→ Marsh Broker→ BuildSure MGA→ UW Review

Scenario 1 — MGA Bind (Score 7): ABC Construction ka experience achha hai, losses nahi hain. MGA ne score 7 diya. Policy MGA ne khud bind ki — $1M GL + $1M Auto. Premium $125,000. Carrier ko sirf monthly bordereau mein pata chala. Tier 1 Authority

Scenario 2 — Refer to Carrier (Score 4): ABC Construction ko $5M limit chahiye jo MGA ke $1M binding limit se bahar hai. MGA ne Refer to Carrier kiya. Carrier ne UW review kiya aur approve kiya. MGA ne bind kiya but Carrier ke approval se. Tier 1 — Outside Binding Limit

Scenario 3 — Decline (Score 2): ABC Construction ki loss history bahut buri hai. MGA ne score 2 diya — Decline. Carrier ko bhi report kiya ki kyun decline kiya.

Moral of the Story: MGA ko Carrier ka trust chahiye. Har decision track hota hai. Bordereau se Carrier monitor karta hai ki MGA sahi decisions le raha hai ya nahi. Agar MGA bar-bar galat decisions lega toh Carrier uska authority wapas le sakta hai.
