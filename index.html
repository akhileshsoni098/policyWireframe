Integration: PAS generates & sends the offer to MGA. The offer then flows MGA → Broker → Insured (tracked automatically). The insured's decision returns to PAS via the MGA; PAS records that response (it never selects Accept/Reject). Policies that reach their expiration date without an accepted renewal auto-expire. Consumes: MGA API, Broker API. Events: NoticeGenerated, NoticeSent, InsuredDecisionRecorded, PolicyExpired.
